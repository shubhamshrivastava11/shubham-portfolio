# Frontend Design Skill

This design skill is intended to guide the website UI toward a premium, modern, enterprise-grade landing page.

## Design tokens
- Color palette: deep slate background, cyan accent, soft violet highlights, white text, and muted slate neutrals.
- Typography: use a restrained scale with Inter for body text and Poppins for display headings.
- Spacing: base spacing unit is 8px. Use consistent padding and margins across sections.
- Border radius: rounded corners should feel smooth and refined (20px–32px on cards and sections).

## Layout principles
- Use clear visual hierarchy with large hero headings, concise subheadings, and distinct content blocks.
- Prefer multi-column grids for feature layouts and testimonials on desktop.
- Maintain generous whitespace around groups and call-to-action areas.

## Component behavior
- Use motion for entrance animation, hover states, and section reveals.
- Apply scroll-triggered fade-up animations for cards, metrics, and testimonials.
- Keep buttons bold and visible: filled accent CTA and subtle outline secondary actions.

## Avoid
- Generic bland AI layouts.
- Random colors or inconsistent spacing.
- Crowded sections without breathing room.

## Voice
- Confident yet polished.
- Focus on AI product leadership, enterprise readiness, and recruiter-friendly storytelling.
